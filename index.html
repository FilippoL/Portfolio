About Me

I'm an AI researcher and software engineer specializing in computer vision, deep learning, and graphics programming. My work bridges academic research with practical engineering, from developing neural networks for remote physiological monitoring to building custom game engines in DirectX and OpenGL.

Currently exploring procedural 3D generation, co-founding PecuHealth and ClusTEQ, and working on cutting-edge embedded systems at Teoresi. My research has been published in academic venues, focusing on contactless health monitoring, NLP bias detection, and ethical AI systems.

2021 Master's Thesis

NrPPG-NNET: An End to End Deep Learning Approach to Remote Heart Rate Estimation Through Spatial Temporal Representation

Filippo Maria Libardi

Utrecht University, November 2021

Deep learning approach to remote heart rate estimation using spatial-temporal representations. Novel methodology for contactless cardiovascular monitoring through computer vision, eliminating the need for traditional touch sensors.

2021 Research Paper

Novel approach to language bias visualization through temporal analysis of large-scale Reddit datasets. Introduces methodology for collecting and analyzing bias scores from text corpora using word embeddings.
